# STORY 6 — Every third drink is free (automatic)

**As a** customer buying several drinks,
**I want** every third unit of the same drink to be free,
**so that** stocking up for a group actually saves me money.

## Description

The "soda trio" deal (ops keeps renaming it): buy 2, get 1 free on
drinks. For every three units of the same drink in the bag, one of them
is free. Three sodas — pay for two. Six sodas — pay for four. Five is the
awkward one; ops decided five pays for four: only complete sets of three
count, no partial credit.

It works per drink type, not mixed — three sodas and three coffees are
two free items, not three. Each free one comes off at that drink's own
price: a free soda is $1.99 off, a free coffee is $1.49 off.

**Milkshakes are excluded from this deal.** They ring at $4.49 and the
margin slide on comping them was, in the ops manager's words, "dark
red". A milkshake rings at full price no matter how many are in the bag;
the trio deal exists for every other drink type.

The deal is automatic, and the epic's one-automatic-deal and best-price
rules apply.
